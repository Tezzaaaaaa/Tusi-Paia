# Tusi Paia

A bilingual (English / Gagana Samoa) historical guide to the Bible.

## What is included

- A historical timeline covering the Hebrew scriptures, Greek translations, early Christian writings, manuscripts, the Latin Vulgate, printing and major English translation milestones.
- Illustrated diagrams explaining the difference between scrolls and codices.
- A side-by-side English and Samoan passage comparison, with selectable examples and translation commentary.
- A Samoan Bible history section covering the London Missionary Society translation work, early printed portions, complete editions, revisions and contemporary translation work.
- Context on canon differences between Jewish, Protestant, Catholic and Eastern Orthodox traditions.
- Links to museum, university-library, scholarly and Bible Society sources.

## Accuracy and scope

Dates are approximate where scholarship or catalogues differ. In particular, sources describe different stages of the early Samoan Bible project (translation, printing, complete edition and later revision), so the page notes the 1860/1862 discrepancy rather than presenting a false certainty.

The passage comparison identifies its editions: King James Version (English) and Samoan Edited Old Version (SOV, 2017). It is a short educational comparison, not a complete Bible text or a critical edition. The site distinguishes historical evidence from theological interpretation.
